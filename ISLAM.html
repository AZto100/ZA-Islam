Key Documented Issues

  • South Africa financing node: UN Monitoring Team and US Treasury have repeatedly flagged SA as a conduit for Islamic State funds to African affiliates (including Mozambique). Multiple individuals sanctioned (e.g. 2022 designations). Cash-intensive informal economy and grey-listing history (FATF) increase vulnerability. No large-scale domestic attacks in recent years, but facilitation risk is real.
  • Mozambique (Cabo Delgado): Active Islamic State Mozambique (ISM) insurgency since ~2017. Thousands killed, hundreds of thousands displaced. Explicit goal of sharia rule in areas of influence. Main kinetic threat in southern Africa.
  • Political advocacy: Fringe parties/groups in South Africa have sought to register platforms that include sharia elements; at least one “Islamic State of Africa”-style application was rejected by the Electoral Commission as incompatible with the secular constitution.
  • Demography: Muslim populations remain small minorities except in Malawi (~13–20%). No evidence of rapid demographic shift toward majority status in the four focus countries.

Counter-measures (standard democratic tools)

  • Strengthen Financial Intelligence Units and AML/CFT enforcement (address FATF gaps).
  • Transparent foreign funding rules for religious and civil-society organisations.
  • Maintain secular constitutional standards for party registration and education curricula.
  • Regional security cooperation on Cabo Delgado and cross-border facilitation networks.
  • Public data transparency: regular publication of demographics, terror designations, and court outcomes.
